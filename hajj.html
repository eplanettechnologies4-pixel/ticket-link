Sacred Pilgrimage

The 5 Holy Days of Hajj (Manasik)

Our senior religious guides accompany every pilgrim group through each obligatory stage.

8th

Day of Tarwiyah

Pilgrims enter Ihram and depart for the tent city of Mina, spending the night in prayer and contemplation.

9th

Day of Arafat & Muzdalifah

The supreme pillar of Hajj: standing at the plain of Arafat until sunset, followed by night prayer and pebble collection at Muzdalifah.

10th

Yawm-un-Nahr (Eid)

Stoning of Jamarat al-Aqaba, animal sacrifice (Qurbani), Halq/Taqsir, and Tawaf al-Ifadah at the Holy Kaaba.

11-13

Ayyam at-Tashreeq

Stay in Mina tents, pebble stoning at all three Jamarat, and concluding with the farewell Tawaf al-Wida.

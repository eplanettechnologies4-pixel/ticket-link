Pilgrim Guidance

The 4 Key Pillars of Umrah

Our experienced scholars and group mentors guide you step-by-step through every ritual.

1

Ihram & Niyyah

Enter the state of Ihram before the designated Miqat with Ghusl, Ihram garments, and recite the Talbiyah (Labbayk Allahumma Labbayk).

2

Tawaf Al-Umrah

Perform 7 anti-clockwise circuits around the Holy Kaaba starting and ending at the Hajr al-Aswad with sincere prayers.

3

Sa'i (Safa & Marwah)

Walk and jog 7 times between Mount Safa and Mount Marwah, commemorating the devotion of Sayyidah Hajar (A.S).

4

Halq or Taqsir

Men shave or trim their hair evenly, and women cut a fingertip length of hair to conclude the sacred Umrah pilgrimage.

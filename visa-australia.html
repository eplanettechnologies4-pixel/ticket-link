Documents Required for Australia

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity)
  • Online application (ImmiAccount) confirmation
  • Recent photographs
  • Bank statements & proof of funds
  • Employment / business proof & tax records
  • Travel itinerary and accommodation details
  • Invitation / sponsor documents (if applicable)

Exact requirements depend on your profile and may change. Our team will confirm the final checklist for you.
